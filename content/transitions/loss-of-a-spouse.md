    - title: "Understanding what your financial life looks like now"
      body: "Income usually drops. Expenses often do not, at least not right away. The tax situation can get harder at the same time, including the transition from married filing jointly to a single filer and what that means for Social Security, RMDs, and Medicare. This is sometimes called the widow's penalty, and most people have no idea it is coming."

      body: "A spouse is often more than a partner. They may have remembered the passwords, managed the accounts, made the appointments, and been the first call when something went wrong. Planning for what comes next includes who helps with decisions now, and whether the estate documents and care plan still make sense for one person instead of two."
